# Minigame 1
## Devlog
Think of a film set: a GameObject is like an actor or prop, and its Components are the equipment and abilities attached to it—a camera, a sound source, or a script for how it behaves. A Scene is the whole set, bringing many actors and props together in one place. Add, remove, or change a Component, and the GameObject can do something different; move GameObjects between Scenes, and they become part of a different setting.
